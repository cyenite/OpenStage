09:00Free church presentation software

Put the words on the wall.

OpenStage puts lyrics, scripture, lower thirds and a stage display on your screens. It is free and open source, and it was made first for bilingual services, modest PCs and volunteers on a Sunday morning.

GPL-3.0, no subscription

    09:18Not in the plan

    The power goes. The service doesn't start over.

    OpenStage saves what is on screen every time it changes: it writes a temporary file, syncs it to disk, then swaps it in, so a half-written save cannot exist. When the PC comes back, every screen picks up where it was. Anything that was deleted in the meantime is quietly dropped.

    If the presentation engine itself crashes, it restarts on its own and reconnects every window. The projector holds its last frame while that happens, so the congregation never sees a desktop.

    09:30Scripture and song

    Two languages. One slide.

    Songs and readings carry a second language line by line. Each theme has its own box for it, with its own font, colour and place on the screen, so English and Swahili sit together without anyone retyping a slide.

    When a song has only one language, the main text grows into the space the translation would have used. One theme works for both.

    PlannedPhones in the pews: the congregation follows along in the language they choose.

    09:45Hand-over

    Hand it to a volunteer. Lock the rest.

    Set a PIN and the window becomes one simple screen: the service, what is showing, what comes next, and big Back, Next and Clear buttons. Nothing can be edited or deleted until someone unlocks it, and the lock stays on after a restart.

    Need a run-through first? Rehearse plays the whole service off-air. The screens keep what they showed, and stopping puts everything back.

    These buttons drive the same projector as the demo at the top. Like the app, this page has one engine and every window follows it.
